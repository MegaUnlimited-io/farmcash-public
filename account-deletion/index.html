Delete Your Account
Follow the steps below to permanently delete your FarmCash account.

You can delete your FarmCash account directly from within the mobile app. The process only takes a minute — here's how.

  1. 1
    Log in to your account in the FarmCash mobile app.

    Open the app and sign in with your email and password.

  2. 2
    Go to Profile.

    Tap the profile icon in the bottom-right corner of the screen.

  3. 3
    Open the Settings tab.

    Inside your profile, tap the Settings tab at the top.

  4. 4
    Tap "I want to delete my account".

    Scroll to the bottom of the Settings page and tap this option.

  5. 5
    Confirm deletion.

    Follow the on-screen prompt to confirm. Once confirmed, your account will be permanently deleted.

⚠️ Important — This cannot be undone

Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. You will forfeit any balance of earnings that remains on your account at the time of deletion. Make sure to redeem any available balance before proceeding.

💬 Need help instead?

If you'd prefer to submit a support ticket for your deletion request, or if you have any questions about the process, tap the Support button on the same Profile > Settings page to get in touch with our team.
