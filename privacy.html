7. Data Protection for Biometric Information

As part of our identity verification process (KYC), we may collect biometric data, such as facial recognition data derived from selfies or live photos. This data is considered highly sensitive, and we take additional precautions to protect it.

7.1 Purpose of Biometric Data Collection

We collect biometric data solely for the following purposes:

  • Verifying your identity before allowing withdrawals
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring that each user operates only one account
  • Complying with anti-money laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) legal requirements

7.2 How Biometric Data is Processed

  • Biometric verification is performed by trusted third-party providers (such as Onfido, Veriff, Jumio, or similar services) who specialize in secure identity verification
  • These providers extract biometric identifiers (e.g., facial geometry) from the selfie you provide and compare them to your government-issued ID
  • We receive only the verification result (pass/fail), a confidence score, and associated metadata (e.g., timestamp, risk signals). We do not receive or store the raw biometric template itself unless required by law
  • Raw images (selfies and ID scans) are typically retained by the verification provider only as long as necessary for verification, compliance audits, and dispute resolution, then securely deleted in accordance with their retention policies

7.3 Retention of Biometric Data

Biometric data and related verification records are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which they were collected:

  • Verification results and risk signals: Retained as long as your account is active and for up to 3 years following account closure for fraud prevention and compliance purposes
  • Raw images and biometric templates: Typically retained by our verification partners for a limited period (often 30-90 days) unless legal obligations require longer retention

You may request deletion of your biometric data by contacting us at privacy@megaunlimited.io. However, please note that deletion may prevent us from processing future withdrawal requests or complying with legal obligations.

7.4 Additional Protections for Illinois Residents

If you are a resident of Illinois, the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA) provides you with additional rights. Under BIPA:

  • We will not collect, capture, or otherwise obtain your biometric data without first providing you with notice and obtaining your written consent
  • Biometric data will be stored, transmitted, and protected using a reasonable standard of care, at least as protective as the manner in which we store, transmit, and protect other confidential and sensitive information
  • We will not sell, lease, trade, or otherwise profit from your biometric data
  • We will permanently destroy biometric data when the initial purpose for collection has been satisfied or within 3 years of your last interaction with us, whichever occurs first, unless a valid legal obligation requires retention
